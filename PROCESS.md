# Process

How I got to this design. Fill in every section, in English. Short and honest beats long and polished.

Put images in the `docs/` folder and show them here like this: `![Old queue screen](docs/old-queue.png)`. Links to Figma, Miro or FigJam are fine too.

---

## 1. Audit of the current tool

What's wrong with the moderation tool today, and why? Screenshots with notes are welcome.

## 2. Who uses it and what they need

Who works in the tool, how often, and what they are trying to get done.

## 3. Mood board and references

Images, products and screens that inspired you. For each one, one line on **why** it is here.

## 4. Sketches, wireframes and dropped ideas

Early sketches and wireframes, including the directions you dropped and why.

## 5. Decision log

The key decisions you made.

| Decision | Options I considered | Why this one |
|---|---|---|
| | | |

## 6. AI log

Which AI tools you used and how. Your key prompts, what worked, what didn't, and what you fixed by hand.

## 7. What's next

What you would do with more time, and how you would test the design with retouchers.
